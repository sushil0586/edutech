# W07 unfamiliar-user observation

Run this with people who have not used the product and without coaching. Record participant role and device/platform, but no names, credentials, answer content or personal data.

| Task | Start | Success condition |
|---|---|---|
| Teacher question find/add | Teacher lands in their workspace | Finds a suitable question and adds it to an exam |
| Student wallet history | Student lands in their workspace | Finds history and explains the latest entry |
| Learner signup/first action | Learner opens signup | Completes profile and reaches the first assigned action |

For each run, record elapsed time, wrong turns, validation or system errors, facilitator intervention, abandonment point and completion. Capture the platform and release shown by the test build. Do not interpret automated browser checks as this human evidence.

The pilot gate can pass when the agreed participant sample is complete, at least 80% finish their core task without intervention, no repeated severe confusion remains, and every observed assessment-blocking defect has an owner and disposition.
