“Launch blocker” is scoped to the launch that depends on that capability. Automated payment and app-store gates need not delay an explicitly assisted web pilot. Tenant access, correct scoring, answer safety and a verified deployed candidate apply to every launch.

How far from market

Start with an assisted institute web pilot around one curriculum and objective assessments. Keep direct consumer payments, broad public-exam access, advanced descriptive moderation and app stores outside that first promise unless their own gates are completed.

Two full-time engineers, dedicated QA and timely founder/content/design decisions; estimates are planning ranges, not delivery commitments.
